# Between the Pages — visual quality standard

Owner's explicit requirements: beautiful premium editorial photographs, matching the recipient's world (Gen Z book lover, modest Muslim identity), not arbitrary stock imagery. The approved gift image is the visual benchmark.

- Photographic, warm, elegant, sophisticated, cozy; no visible faces.
- Focus on reading, books, tasteful stationery, coffee/tea, modest fabrics, natural light, linen, olive, ivory, espresso and soft gold.
- No random decor with no relationship to reading. No Christmas visuals.
- No illegible text, foreign book titles or invented scripture embedded in decorative pictures. Use real interface text in English or Dutch.
- Real library book covers and user-uploaded photos are exempt from decorative typography rules.
- Ramadan daytime imagery must not feature drinks in use. Eid and Ramadan imagery should be tasteful and accurate, never fabricated religious text.
- Use reviewed, fixed local assets rather than unverified remote stock. Inspect each actual image and its mobile and desktop crop before approving.
- Preserve twelve distinct month images and all approved app features. Do not equate a source URL or successful code test with visual approval.
- Status: gift cover approved by owner; home cover newly generated and visually inspected; generic tiles temporarily reuse these two local photographs. Remaining monthly and seasonal photos require individual review and replacement before gift delivery.
